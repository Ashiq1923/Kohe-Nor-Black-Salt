Pure and Ancient

Our salt comes from the Khewra mine in Pakistan's Salt Range, one of the largest salt mines in the world.

The rock salt here formed in deposits laid down hundreds of millions of years ago. It is cut from the mine by hand, sorted by colour and crystal, then processed in our own facility into black salt with its deep colour and mineral aroma.

Nothing is added to make it look better. What you open in the jar is what came out of the mountain.

  • Khewra, PunjabMined in the Salt Range, Jhelum district
  • Hand-selectedRock graded by colour and crystal before processing
  • UnrefinedNo bleaching, no anti-caking agents
